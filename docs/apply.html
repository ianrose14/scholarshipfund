How to Apply

2027 applications open January 1, 2027

Applications are due by Sunday, April 18, 2027 at 11:59 PM Eastern.


Start or Continue Your Application

Sign in with your email address. Your progress is saved as you go.

1

Check Your Eligibility

Review the eligibility requirements and contact us with any questions.

2

Fill Out the Application Form

A short online form with your contact details and NNP program information.

3

Upload a Personal Statement and Resume

Your personal statement should cover why you're pursuing an NNP degree, your nursing experience, and your financial need.

4

Get a Financial Aid Certification

Ask the financial aid office at your NNP program to complete and sign this form, then upload it. It's fine if you haven't been admitted yet.

5

Request Letters of Recommendation

Two letters are required, at least one from a current or recent supervisor. We'll email your recommenders a link to upload their letters, so ask them early.

6

Schedule an Interview

Qualified candidates will be contacted by email to schedule an interview.
